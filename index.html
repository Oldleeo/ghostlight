    How the ghost light works.
    
    
    
    
    
      
    1 · Sweep
    The image is cut into tiles. Flat tiles (no edges) are skipped — there is no text to haunt. In every other tile, pixels are split into two luminance clusters: ink and paper.
    
      
    2 · Measure
    Ink and paper are compared with the WCAG 2.2 contrast formula. Ratios are floored, never rounded up: a 4.497 is a ghost, not a pass. Adjacent failing tiles merge into one haunting.
    
      
    3 · Exorcise
    The text colour is moved through OKLCH lightness only, in the smallest step that clears the target, keeping hue and chroma. If no lightness works, it falls back to black or white.
    
      
    4 · Possession
    Machado (2009) colour-vision matrices in linear RGB simulate protanopia, deuteranopia and tritanopia; achromatopsia uses luminance; low vision applies blur. A pair can pass for you and haunt someone else.
    
    
    
    
    Ghostlight is a design aid, not a certification. WCAG contrast is one part of legibility — size, weight and real users matter too. Built with Kiro by Oldlee for Kiroween 2026 · MIT licensed.
